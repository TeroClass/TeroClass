Join by link Who can join this unit

Off (recommended): only the people you invite can join. On: whoever opens and creates an account becomes a teacher of this unit, uses a seat of your plan and sees the unit's AI settings and playbooks. Turn it on only for a short time and share the link only with your team.

Recording consent of minors Students under 18

Off: a student under 18 writes their guardian's name and email and declares that the guardian agrees (TeroClass does not verify it; you see it as "declared by the student"). On: your institution declares that it already collected the written authorization of the families of its students under 18 (for example on paper), so they are not asked for their guardian's details. Applies to this unit and everything below it.

School year Group notebooks and boards

Each group has one notebook and one board per school year, with a section (and a page) per class. By default the school year is the calendar year (January to December); choose March, for example, for a school year from March to February. Applies to this unit and everything below it (a unit below can choose its own).

AI engine
Class plans are generated with Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o), managed centrally by TeroClass. No keys or setup needed for your institution or teachers.

Choose which bars teachers see in Smart tools → Learning approach. Units below inherit your choice: they can hide bars or add their own.
