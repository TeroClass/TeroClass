Upload the documents that define your institution: principles, pedagogical model, values, non-negotiables. The AI extracts your core and applies it to every class your teachers generate.
📘 Upload playbook
PDF, DOCX, TXT · Max 15MB each · You can upload several
AI engine
Class plans are generated with Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o), managed centrally by Tero Class. No keys or setup needed for your institution or teachers.
🎚️ Learning approach bars
Choose which bars teachers see in Smart tools → Learning approach. Units below inherit your choice: they can hide bars or add their own.
🌳 Structure
Your institution and its units: main institution › campus / faculty › department / area. Open a unit to work on it (dashboard, branding, AI settings). Everything you set in a unit applies to the units below it.
