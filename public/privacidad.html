2. Quién decide sobre tus datos

  • Si usás TeroClass a través de una institución educativa (colegio, liceo, instituto, universidad): la institución es la responsable de la base de datos de sus docentes y estudiantes, y TeroClass actúa como encargado de tratamiento, siguiendo sus instrucciones (contrato de encargado / DPA con cada institución).

4. Menores de edad

Muchos estudiantes son menores. Para que se graben las clases de una persona menor de 18 años hace falta la autorización de su madre, padre o tutor/a: el estudiante indica el nombre y el email de su tutor/a y declara que está de acuerdo. TeroClass no verifica esa declaración (la institución la ve como "declarado por el estudiante"); la verificación del tutor/a por email está pendiente. Una institución también puede declarar que ya juntó por su cuenta la autorización escrita de las familias (por ejemplo, en papel): en ese caso no se piden los datos del tutor/a y la responsabilidad de esas autorizaciones es de la institución. En España, la edad para consentir por sí mismo es 14 años (art. 7 LOPDGDD) [revisar con el abogado cómo aplicarlo]. Los insights de un estudiante solo los ven él o ella, sus docentes y los admins de su institución.

Privacy policy

Draft version · last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

TeroClass is a platform to plan classes with AI, teach online and see what was learned in each class. It is operated by [RAZÓN SOCIAL], tax id [RUT], address [DIRECCIÓN] ("TeroClass", "we"). Privacy contact: [EMAIL DE CONTACTO].

2. Who decides about your data

When you use TeroClass through an educational institution, the institution is the controller of its teachers' and students' data and TeroClass is its processor (data processing agreement with each institution). For personal accounts (no institution), TeroClass is the controller.

3. Data we process

Account data (name, e-mail, hashed password, role, institution); the classes you plan; students loaded by the institution (name, e-mail), groups, calendar and attendance; the shared class document; class audio (once the live classroom is connected) and AI insights — only with consent, which can be withdrawn at any time; the recording consent and, if the person chose to write it, the reason for declining; technical data (IP in server logs, application errors, AI usage without the text of the requests). We don't sell data, we don't use it for advertising, there is no emotion detection and no voice identification.

4. Minors

A minor's classes are recorded only if the student gives their parent's or guardian's name and e-mail and declares that the guardian agrees. TeroClass does not verify that declaration (the institution sees it as "declared by the student"); verifying the guardian by e-mail is pending. An institution may instead declare that it already collected the families' written authorization itself (e.g. on paper); then no guardian details are asked and the institution is responsible for those authorizations. In Spain the age of digital consent is 14 [to review].

5. Processors

Supabase (database and accounts, Brazil – São Paulo); Microsoft Azure OpenAI (AI, United States – East US 2; not used to train models); Vercel (hosting, United States / global); LiveKit (live video, when connected); Resend (e-mail, when enabled); Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection, if enabled); Sentry (error reports, if enabled). International transfers rely on each provider's contractual safeguards [to review].

6. Retention

Original class audio: deleted after 30 days. Account, saved classes and insights: while the account or the institution's contract is active, then [period to define]. Technical and security logs: [period to define]. Pending invitations expire after 14 days.

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection and (EU) restriction and portability. From your account menu you can download your data and delete your account; otherwise write to [EMAIL DE CONTACTO]. You may complain to the URCDP (Uruguay) or the AEPD (Spain).

8. Browser storage

No advertising or third-party cookies. Your browser stores your session and a few preferences (language, selected unit) in local storage, which the app needs to work.
